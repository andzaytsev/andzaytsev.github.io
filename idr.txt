[IL, USA] Employment discrimination based on immigration status

I am an international student at a public university in Illinois. I am starting the process of adjusting my status to permanent resident (getting sponsored by my future spouse). I am applying for full-time jobs after college, however I will not get my green card until about a month before my graduation.

Most of the recruitment (including interviews) at my university is done through the university career system. However, the system has immigration status for every student, which is shown to the student and the companies in the personal profile and can only be changed by the university. Based on that, a lot of companies select only certain categories of people who can apply for a job/an interview. Since my status is still shown as an international student, I cannot apply for a lot of jobs (it simply says I am not eligible to apply), even though I will have permanent employment authorization when I start working.

I have tried talking to the career center at the university, but they said that they would only change it if I bring them my green card, which I do not have yet. I believe this is discrimination based on immigration status, which is illegal under the INA (Immigration and Nationality Act) and the IRCA (Immigration Reform and Control Act): http://www.workplacefairness.org/immigration-status. (As far as I understand, the only time I can be asked for any documentation is when filling out form I-9.) While I mentioned that to the career center employees, they only took my name and my phone number and said someone would contact me eventually.

What would be the best action for me to take? Should I contact the Office of the Special Council as suggested in the link above (is it applicable in this situation)? Can I pursue legal action and sue the university for discrimination? I feel I am illegally discriminated against, and it hinders my employment opportunities.

TL;DR: University career system allows employers to prevent people from applying based on immigration status, because of which I am discriminated against. What should I do?
