Score Assured
Score Assured is a British startup software company that provides landlords with a report that is supposed to help them decide whether someone would be a good tenant. After applying for a property, a prospective tenant is sent an “opt-in” link that, when clicked, gives Score Assured full access to their social media profiles (Twitter, LinkedIn, Facebook, etc.). After scraping the profiles and sending the data through analytical software, the company compiles a report detailing factors like personality and “financial stress level” that the landlord can use to determine whether or not to rent to the prospective tenant.
Questions
In the course of your argument, you should address the following questions.
Is the data involved PII, NPI, or PPI? Descriptively private or normatively private?
Does the way Score Assured uses data violate norms of appropriateness or of distribution?
How do the privacy concerns in this scenario relate to questions of data merging, matching, and mining?
Do you believe the data involved is best protected through informed consent, industry self-regulation, or legislation?
